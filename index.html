Italy Family Trip

Naples • Sorrento & Amalfi Coast • Rome
Culture & Dining Schedule
  • Lunch: Typically 12:30 PM – 3:00 PM.
  • Dinner: From 8:00 PM onward.
  • Mid-Afternoon Gap: Unlikely to find many sit-down restaurants open outside lunch and dinner shifts.
  • Coffee Bar Etiquette: Stand at the counter (al banco) for a quick espresso — local custom and significantly cheaper than table service.
Things to Try
Baladin:
Italian craft beer similar to ale.
Aperol:
Italian bitter apéritif liqueur (Aperol Spritz).
Sfogliatella:
Classic shell-shaped Neapolitan breakfast pastry.
Montanara:
Deep-fried street pizza topped with tomato & cheese.
Delizia al Limone & Limoncello:
Amalfi Coast lemon sponge dessert & lemon liqueur.
Family Ideas & Connectivity
  • Split Amalfi Coast Day: Some family members (Mom / hikers) can hike the Path of the Gods (Bomerano to Nocelle) while others scooter or ferry along the coastline and reunite in Positano or Amalfi.
  • eSIM Option — Holafly: Recommended unlimited-data eSIM for Italy so everyone has reliable maps, translation, and QR ticket access on the move.
